RESEARCH AT A GLANCE

Following pollution from observation to impact

My current research combines satellite measurements, emission estimation, and evaluation against independent data. The NASA FINESST project also explores links to asthma-related health risks.

01

Observe

Use satellite measurements to see how air pollution changes across regions and over time.

02

Estimate

Combine satellite observations, wind data, and computational methods to estimate emissions.

03

Evaluate

Compare estimates with reported emissions and explore implications for air quality and health.
