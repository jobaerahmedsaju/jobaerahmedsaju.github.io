Ph.D. Researcher — Environmental & Water Resources Engineering

Estimating NOx emissions from satellite observations.

I'm a Ph.D. researcher at the University at Buffalo, working with Prof. Kang Sun on physics-based methods for turning satellite column measurements — TROPOMI, TEMPO — into high-resolution NOx emission estimates.

The goal is finer-grained air-quality monitoring: seeing emissions change hour to hour and street to street, not just city to city.

High-resolution NOx

02 — instrument

TEMPO & TROPOMI

Working with geostationary (TEMPO) and low-Earth-orbit (TROPOMI/Sentinel-5P) observations to resolve emissions in time and space.

03 — application

Air quality & health

Connecting improved emissions estimates to air-quality monitoring and public-health assessment in the United States.
