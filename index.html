Satellites · Emissions · Air Quality

Understanding air pollution through satellite observations.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Environmental and Water Resources Engineering at the University at Buffalo. My current work focuses on high-resolution NOx emission estimation from satellite observations for improved air-quality monitoring and health-risk assessment in the United States.

Current focus

Satellite-based emissions research

My doctoral research connects satellite observations, atmospheric data analysis, and emissions estimation to improve how NOx pollution is monitored at high spatial and temporal resolution.
