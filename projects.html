High-Resolution NOx Emission Estimation From TEMPO Observations: Advancing Air Pollution Monitoring and Public Health Analysis

The NASA-funded project estimates NOx emissions from TEMPO measurements, develops and evaluates improved estimation methods, and aims to investigate their relationship with asthma-related health outcomes in New York State.

Research outputs

Published and publicly available work related to the research:

  • Hourly Nitrogen Oxides Emissions Estimated From TEMPO and Comparison With Facility-Level Monitoring Data (peer-reviewed journal article, 2025)
  • Deep Learning-Based Source and Sink Apportionment for Nitrogen Oxides Using the Directional Derivatives of TROPOMI Observations (2026 preprint; manuscript under review at JGR: Machine Learning and Computation)
  • A Physics-Based Neural Network Framework for Estimating Spatiotemporal NOx Reactivity From Satellite Observations (research poster, 2026)
  • Hourly Nitrogen Oxides Emissions Estimated From TEMPO and Comparison With Facility-Level Monitoring Data (science-team poster, 2025)
